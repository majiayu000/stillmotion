# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer through a [private security advisory](https://github.com/majiayu000/stillmotion/security/advisories/new) or by contacting [@majiayu000](https://github.com/majiayu000) on GitHub. All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behavior violates this code.

The full text, including enforcement guidelines, is available at the link above.
